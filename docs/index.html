Open it

Mac: unzip, then right-click the app → Open (only the first time — this bypasses macOS's "unidentified developer" warning; a plain double-click will refuse to run it once). If that doesn't offer an Open option, or you already clicked through and it still won't launch, go to Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to the McGoverner warning — then launch it once more.

Windows: run the installer and click through the wizard (it installs for just your account, no admin needed). SmartScreen will likely warn about an unrecognized publisher the first time — click More info → Run anyway. It adds a Start Menu shortcut you can also pin or send to your desktop.

Your browser opens on its own once it's ready.

Always reopen this same app to launch it. That's how your data stays put and updates arrive automatically — downloading a fresh copy each time starts you over.
